Worked as **Game Programmer**  
*2025 April - 2025 June*

This game was developed during a 2 month 3D game project assignment at Yrgo. You play as Anders investigating the house of your missing grandpa, along the way you find a living camera with unique abilities. Control the camera as a separate character and take photos with different filters in order to solve puzzles. Follow the breadcrumbs left in this wacky mansion filled with sentient appliances.
